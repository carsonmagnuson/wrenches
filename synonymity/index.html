Synonymity

Seven words. Give a synonym for each. The rarer your synonym, the more it's worth.

How scoring works

Each accepted synonym is scored by obscurity, the same formula as Roborean: 8 − log10(frequency per billion words), using Datamuse frequency data. Common words score about 2–3 and rare ones 6 or more. Points are obscurity × 10.

A true synonym (Datamuse rel_syn) gets full points. A close-in-meaning word (ml) gets half. You get 3 tries per word. Real words that don't match don't use up a try. Answering with the prompt word or one of its forms isn't allowed.
