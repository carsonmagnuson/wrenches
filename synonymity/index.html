Synonymity

Seven words. Give a synonym for each. The rarer your synonym, the more it's worth.

How scoring works

Each word has a curated answer list. Claude drafts a broad list of acceptable synonyms (common, literary, archaic, slang), and Datamuse supplies how often each one appears in English.

Answers are scored by obscurity, the same formula as Roborean: 8 − log10(frequency per billion words). Common words score about 2–3 and rare ones 6 or more. Points are obscurity × 10. An exact synonym gets full points and a close-in-meaning one gets half.

You get 3 tries per word. Answering with the prompt word or one of its forms isn't allowed.
